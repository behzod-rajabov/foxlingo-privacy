Account & Data Deletion

At Foxlingo, you have full control over your account and personal data. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time using one of the methods below.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: From within the app

1
Open Foxlingo Launch the app and make sure you are logged in.
2
Go to Profile Tap your profile icon in the bottom navigation bar.
3
Open Settings Tap the gear icon or "Settings" option.
4
Select "Delete Account" Scroll down to find the "Delete Account" option and tap it.
5
Confirm deletion Read the confirmation message and confirm. Your account will be scheduled for deletion.

Option 2: Via email

Send an email to support@foxlingo.app with the subject line "Delete My Account" from the email address associated with your Foxlingo account. We will process your request within 7 business days.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Data Type Action Timeframe
Account information (name, email) Deleted Within 30 days
Learning progress & course history Deleted Within 30 days
Streaks, XP, and achievements Deleted Within 30 days
App settings and preferences Deleted Within 30 days
Notification history Deleted Within 30 days
Diagnostic & crash logs Retained (anonymized) Up to 90 days
Transaction/payment records (if any) Retained As required by law (up to 7 years)

⚠️ Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your progress, streaks, and achievements will be permanently lost. We recommend exporting any important data before proceeding.

Partial Data Deletion

If you wish to delete specific data without deleting your account (for example, clearing your learning history or resetting your progress), you can contact us at support@foxlingo.app with your specific request. We will respond within 7 business days.
